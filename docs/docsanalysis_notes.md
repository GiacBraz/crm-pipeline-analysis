Cycle = close\_date - engage\_date, in days. Based on 4,238 Won deals.



Overall

\- Median 57 days, mean 51.78.

\- 1114 deals (26%) closed in 0-9 days.

\- The mean is below the median because of this block of very fast closings.

| product | n\_deals | median | mean |

| GTX Pro | 729 | 47 | 48.33 |

\- Most products sit between 56 and 61 days (median). GTX Pro is the fastest at 47.

\- GTK 500 is the slowest, but it has only 15 Won deals, so the figure is not reliable.



By agent

\- 30 agents. Won deals per agent range from 55 to 349.

\- Fastest median: Elease Gluck, 17.5 days (80 deals).

\- Slowest median: Moses Frase, 78 days (129 deals).

\- The spread between agents (17.5 to 78 days) is much wider than between products (47 to 61, GTK 500 excluded).



Fastest vs slowest agent, by product

\- Product mix does not explain the difference between the two agents. Other possible causes were not tested.





Q4 - Stalled open deals



Age of an open deal = days from engage\_date to 2017-12-31. Only Engaging deals are included (1,589). Prospecting deals have no engage\_date, so they have no age.



\*\*Threshold 1: median cycle of Won deals (57 days)\*\*

\- 1,537 of 1,589 open deals (96.7%) are older than 57 days.

\- Every agent has between 92% and 100% of open deals flagged, so this threshold does not separate agents.



\*\*Win rate by cycle length (closed deals only)\*\*

Overall win rate is 63.2% (4,238 Won, 2,473 Lost).



| days | deals | win rate |

|---|---|---|

| 0-9 | 2,029 | 54.9% |

| 10-19 | 1,044 | 60.7% |

| 20-59 | 622 | 63.9% to 78.0% |

| 60-129 | 2,979 | 66.3% to 74.8% |

| 130+ | 58 | 75.9% |



\- The win rate is lowest for deals closed in under 20 days.

\- After 20 days it stays between 64% and 78%. A longer cycle does not mean a higher risk of losing.

\- This only covers closed deals. Open deals that never close are not in these numbers.

\- Buckets 20-29, 30-39 and 130+ have fewer than 100 deals each.



\*\*Age of open deals\*\*

\- Ages go from 9 to 423 days. There is no clear gap that separates "normal" from "old" deals.

\- Peak at 150-179 days: 438 deals (27.6%).

\- Only 211 open deals (13.3%) are younger than 120 days.

\- The longest closed deal (Won or Lost) took 138 days. 1,291 open deals (81.2%) are older than that.



\*\*Threshold 2: 200 days (business choice)\*\*

\- The data does not give a natural threshold, so 200 days (about 6-7 months) is a business decision.

\- 649 open deals (40.8%) are older than 200 days.



\*\*By agent (200 days)\*\*

\- 17 agents have between 46.6% and 64.0% of open deals stalled.

\- 10 agents have 0% stalled.

\- The 10 agents at 0% are all in the Central region, under two managers: Melvin Marxen (5) and Dustin Brinkmann (5).

\- Not tested: why Central has no old deals, and whether product explains the difference.
